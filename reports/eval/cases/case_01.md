# Case 01 — lang=en, kind=explanation, article=Article 775

## PROMPT
What does Article 775 of the Egyptian Civil Code establish? Summarize it for a non-lawyer.

## ORIGINAL ARTICLE (EN)
Suretyship may be given without the knowledge and 
even in spite of the opposition of the debtor.

## ORIGINAL ARTICLE (AR)
تجوز كفالة المدين بغير عمله ، وتجوز أيضا رغم معارضته .

## REFERENCE (gold-polished by Gemini/Claude)
Article 775 of the Egyptian Civil Code states that suretyship can validly arise without the debtor's knowledge — and even against the debtor's express opposition — recognizing that the protective relationship is fundamentally between the creditor and the surety, not between the debtor and the surety.

Article 775 of the Egyptian Civil Code provides:

* **Surety can be given without the debtor's knowledge:** A third party can validly stand as surety for a debt without ever informing the debtor; the debtor's consent is not a precondition for valid suretyship.
* **Surety can be given despite the debtor's opposition:** Even if the debtor expressly objects to the suretyship, it remains valid; the debtor cannot block a friendly third party from securing his obligation.
* **The relationship is creditor-surety, not debtor-surety:** The contract of suretyship is between the surety and the creditor; the debtor's role is the underlying obligation, not a party to the security arrangement.
* **Practical effect: protects creditor and surety:** This rule lets a creditor accept additional security without negotiation friction with the debtor, and lets a surety voluntarily protect a friend or family member without asking permission.

Example: A father wants to support his estranged son's business loan and offers to guarantee it to the bank. The son refuses to ask for help. Under Article 775, the father can still validly bind himself as surety to the bank — the son's refusal does not prevent the father's legal commitment.

## MODEL PREDICTION
Article 775 of the Egyptian Civil Code establishes that a person who has been injured in an accident caused by another's negligence is entitled to compensation from the insurer of the negligent party.

Article 775 of the Egyptian Civil Code provides:

* **Who is entitled to compensation:** A person who has been injured in an accident caused by another's negligence.
* **Who pays the compensation:** The insurer of the negligent party.
* **Why the insurer pays:** Because the insurer is the one who paid for the insurance coverage that made the other party liable in the first place.

Example: A pedestrian is hit by a car driven by a driver who was speeding and driving under the influence of drugs. Under Article 775, the pedestrian can sue the insurer of the speeding driver for the damages he sustained. The insurer will pay because they covered the driver when he was insured.
